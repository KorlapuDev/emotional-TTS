Iske baad wo Heiter aur Eisen se milte hain. Heiter ab Holy City ka bishop ban chuka hai aur bohot budha lag raha hai, jabki dwarf hone ki wajah se Eisen ke looks mein koi khas badlaav nahi aaya tha. Frieren unhe batati hai ki meteors ko sahi jagah se dekhne ke liye unhe ek hafta chalna padega, jo sunkar Himmel thoda niraash (disappoint) hota hai. Par safar ke dauran Himmel apne purane dino ko yaad karta hai aur batata hai ki usne apne doston ke sath safar karna kitna miss kiya tha. Aakhirkaar wo ek vishal jheel (giant lake) ke paas pohanchte hain jahan aasman bilkul saaf hota hai. Meteors ka nazara sach mein jadui tha. Himmel aasman ko dekhte hue Frieren ko is aakhiri aur shandar safar ke liye dil se shukriya kehta hai.
